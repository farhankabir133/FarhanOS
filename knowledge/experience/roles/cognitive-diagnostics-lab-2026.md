# Architect & Researcher — Cognitive Diagnostics Lab (2026)

## Overview
Lead research and architecture work on large language model evaluation for automated cognitive health screenings and behavioral diagnostics.

## Responsibilities
- Architecting clinical NLP pipelines
- Evaluating LLMs for cognitive health screening
- Behavioral diagnostics from text and multimodal signals
- Researching mental-health text analysis

## Business Impact
- Enable scalable, automated cognitive health assessments
- Reduce clinical screening costs and turnaround time
- Improve accessibility to behavioral health diagnostics

## Technologies Used
- Python, PyTorch, BERT, Transformers
- LLM prompting and evaluation frameworks
- React, FastAPI, Node.js

## Engineering Contributions
- Designed multi-layer NLP evaluation harnesses
- Built prompt injection and perplexity detection frameworks
- Developed multimodal emotion recognition systems

## Challenges
- Ensuring model robustness across diverse patient populations
- Balancing sensitivity and specificity in clinical predictions
- Maintaining low false-positive rates in real-world deployments

## Key Projects
- RankFlow AI — ML pipeline platform
- OMNIVA AI — Enterprise LLM observability
- codelab-ai-genkit-rag — RAG starter kit

## Lessons Learned
- Clinical NLP requires rigorous validation and domain-specific tuning
- Intervention design in AI must account for real-world deployment constraints
- Integration of LLM monitoring with model governance frameworks is essential
