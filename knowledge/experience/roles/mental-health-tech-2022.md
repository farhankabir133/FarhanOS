# Full Stack Engineer & NLP Researcher — Mental Health Tech (2022)

## Overview
Full Stack Engineer & NLP Researcher focused on mental health applications using NLP and machine learning.

## Responsibilities
- Develop full-stack applications with React and Node.js
- Research and implement NLP models for mental health detection
- Analyze clinical text data (Reddit, patient transcripts)
- Collaborate with researchers and clinicians

## Business Impact
- Published 2 peer-reviewed papers (IEEE ICCIT 2023, IEEE ECCE 2025)
- Developed clinically validated depression and emotion detection models
- Built scalable web applications reaching clinical communities

## Technologies Used
- React, Node.js, Python, FastAPI
- BERT, RoBERTa, Wav2Vec 2.0
- PostgreSQL, Docker, GCP

## Engineering Contributions
- RoBERTa-based depression detection (F1: 0.914)
- BERT/RoBERTa emotion classifiers (F1: 0.908)
- Spectrogram-based multimodal emotion recognition (92.3% accuracy)

## Challenges
- Class imbalance in clinical datasets
- Ethical AI considerations in mental health
- Model generalization across populations and platforms

## Key Projects
- Multimodal Emotion Recognizer — Bimodal fusion system
- Depression Detection paper — RoBERTa on clinical Reddit data

## Lessons Learned
- Clinical NLP requires rigorous validation and ethics review
- Transfer learning from general BERT models needs domain-specific fine-tuning
- Cross-modal fusion requires careful feature alignment
