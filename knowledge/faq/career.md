# FAQ — Career & Experience

## Q: Where does Farhan currently work?
A: Architect & Researcher at Cognitive Diagnostics Lab (2026-present), where he leads LLM evaluation and cognitive health screening research.

## Q: What was Farhan's previous role?
A: Senior AI Developer at Synthetix Solutions (2024), building production NLP and computer vision systems for candidate evaluation.

## Q: What was his first professional role?
A: Full Stack Engineer & NLP Researcher at Mental Health Tech (2022), where he built clinical NLP pipelines and published his first IEEE paper on depression detection.

## Q: How many publications does he have?
A: 4 peer-reviewed publications at IEEE ICCIT and IEEE ECCE, covering LLM safety, AI interview systems, emotion detection, and depression detection.

## Q: What research topics does he focus on?
A: LLM evaluation, adversarial robustness, multimodal emotion recognition, depression detection from social media, and cognitive health screening.

## Q: How many products has he shipped?
A: 12+ products including FarhanOS, RankFlow AI, OMNIVA AI, TypeRush, The Ink Home, SafeSide Predictor, Exam-Survival, BuildSafe, Buddy-Script, AutoSpark, and more.

## Q: What was his career path?
A: Independent builder (2020) → Full-stack eng + NLP researcher (2022) → Senior AI developer (2024) → Architect & researcher (2026).

## Q: Does he have industry or academic experience?
A: Both. He has published in peer-reviewed venues and built products with real users in enterprise, health, sports, and education domains.

## Q: Has he led teams or projects?
A: He has led engineering initiatives and research projects, though most recent work is as an individual contributor with high ownership.

## Q: What are his long-term goals?
A: To advance AI safety in clinical diagnostics, build trustworthy LLM evaluation frameworks, and continue shipping AI-native products.
