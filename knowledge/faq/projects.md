# FAQ — Projects

## Q: What projects is Farhan most proud of?
A: FarhanOS (his interactive portfolio OS), RankFlow AI (ML benchmarking), OMNIVA AI (enterprise LLM observability), and the Depression Detection paper (F1: 0.914) represent his proudest intersections of research, engineering, and impact.

## Q: What was his first major project?
A: AutoSpark (2024), a no-code workflow automation platform reaching 3.6k+ workflows. It taught him async queueing, GraphQL, and non-technical user experience.

## Q: Does he prefer frontend or backend?
A: He prefers full-stack ownership but leans toward backend architecture and AI integration. He loves building immersive frontends when the UX calls for it (e.g., FarhanOS, The Ink Home).

## Q: What is the most technically complex project?
A: OMNIVA AI, because it combines Go high-throughput ingestion, TensorFlow classification, Prometheus metrics, and enterprise access control in a single observability platform.

## Q: Which project had the biggest user impact?
A: BuildSafe (1.2k+ safety reports) and Exam-Survival (+23% score improvement) both reached real users with measurable outcomes. AutoSpark's 3.6k+ workflows show sustained usage.

## Q: What is FarhanOS?
A: FarhanOS is an immersive portfolio OS built with React, TailwindCSS, Framer Motion, Three.js, and Express. It features a CRT terminal boot loader, 3D environments, and a floating Groq-powered AI assistant with RAG.

## Q: What tech does he use for AI projects?
A: PyTorch for training, BERT/RoBERTa/Hugging Face for NLP, LangChain for RAG, Groq API for low-latency inference, and FastAPI for serving.

## Q: What tech does he use for frontend?
A: React 18/19, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Three.js, Monaco Editor, and Vite.

## Q: What tech does he use for backend?
A: Node.js, Express, FastAPI, Go, Redis, BullMQ, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and Docker.

## Q: What is TypeRush?
A: TypeRush is a typing survival game with real-time Web Audio synthesis, adaptive visual themes, and Groq AI hints. Built with React 19, TailwindCSS, Express, and Firebase.

## Q: What is The Ink Home?
A: An immersive 3D spatial publication portal syncing Medium RSS feeds into interactive WebGL carousels using Three.js and Framer Motion.

## Q: What is SafeSide Predictor?
A: A football analytics command center that simulates match outcomes using Poisson distribution models, with Groq AI commentary and Recharts visualization.

## Q: What is RankFlow AI?
A: An ML pipeline platform for automated model ranking and benchmarking. It has evaluated 840+ models using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, and React.

## Q: What is OMNIVA AI?
A: An enterprise LLM observability platform monitoring 40+ production models with Prometheus, Grafana, and TensorFlow-based output classification.

## Q: What is Buddy-Script?
A: An in-browser AI coding companion using Monaco Editor, React, and a Node.js WebSocket server streaming Groq AI suggestions with 89.4% accuracy.

## Q: What is Exam-Survival?
A: An adaptive exam prep PWA using spaced repetition (SM-2) and Groq AI tutoring, delivering +23% average score improvement.

## Q: What is BuildSafe?
A: A construction safety compliance platform processing 1.2k+ reports with PostGIS geospatial mapping, built with React, Supabase, and Express.

## Q: What is codelab-ai-genkit-rag?
A: A production-ready RAG starter kit with 96.1% chunking accuracy using LangChain, ChromaDB, FastAPI, and Next.js.

## Q: What is AutoSpark?
A: A no-code workflow automation platform with a visual builder, BullMQ async jobs, and GraphQL APIs. It has powered 3.6k+ workflows.

## Q: What is the Multimodal Emotion Recognizer?
A: A bimodal fusion system aligning pitch spectrograms with BERT embeddings for emotion recognition, achieving 92.3% accuracy on 5 emotional states.

## Q: What awards or recognition has he received?
A: 15+ professional certifications, 4 IEEE publications, and product adoption across thousands of real users. No traditional "awards" listed; recognition is through user impact and peer-reviewed publication.

## Q: Does he contribute to open source?
A: Yes, early career focused on open-source contributions through Farhan Lab. He continues to share via GitHub and public projects.

## Q: What is his favorite project to demo?
A: FarhanOS, because it combines portfolio storytelling, 3D graphics, terminal aesthetics, and a functional AI assistant into a single memorable experience.

## Q: What project taught him the most?
A: AutoSpark taught async architecture and non-technical UX. The Depression Detection paper taught clinical rigor and research writing. OMNIVA AI taught enterprise observability.

## Q: What does he do for project ideation?
A: He looks for intersection of emerging tech, real user pain, and measurable outcomes. He prototypes fast, validates with users, then doubles down on what shows promise.

## Q: How does he document projects?
A: Every project has a structured markdown document with YAML frontmatter: overview, problem, solution, architecture, tech stack, features, challenges, trade-offs, lessons learned, and future roadmap.

## Q: Does he use CI/CD?
A: Yes. Vercel for frontend deployments, GitHub Actions for CI, and Docker for reproducible ML environments.

## Q: What hosting platforms does he use?
A: Vercel (primary), Railway (backend), GCP (ML workloads), and self-hosted Docker for some services.

## Q: How does he test his projects?
A: Jest/Vitest for frontend, pytest for Python, integration tests for API routes, and manual testing with observability dashboards for ML components.

## Q: What is his deployment strategy?
A: Serverless-first with Vercel for frontends, containerized workers for async tasks, and cloud runtimes (GCP/Railway) for ML and high-throughput services.
