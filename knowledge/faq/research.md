# FAQ — Research & Publications

## Q: How many peer-reviewed publications does Farhan have?
A: 4 publications at IEEE ICCIT and IEEE ECCE covering LLM safety, interview evaluation, emotion detection, and depression detection.

## Q: What are his research interests?
A: LLM evaluation, adversarial robustness, multimodal emotion recognition, depression detection from social media, and cognitive health screening.

## Q: What was his first publication?
A: "Depression Detection From Social Media Textual Data" at IEEE ICCIT 2023, using RoBERTa on clinical Reddit data with F1: 0.914.

## Q: What is his highest-impact result?
A: The depression detection model achieved F1: 0.914, Sensitivity: 0.893, and Specificity: 0.942 on clinical Reddit data.

## Q: What models did he use for emotion detection?
A: Fine-tuned BERT and RoBERTa for emotion classification from text, achieving RoBERTa F1: 0.908 on clinical emotional states.

## Q: What is the adversarial prompt detection paper about?
A: "Did the Prompt Break the Model?" proposes a perplexity-based framework to detect adversarial prompt injection attacks on LLMs, published at IEEE ICCIT 2025.

## Q: What is the live interview system paper about?
A: "AI-Driven Live Interview System" proposes a multimodal framework combining Wav2Vec 2.0, BERT, and MediaPipe for real-time candidate evaluation during live interviews.

## Q: What is the multimodal emotion recognizer?
A: A bimodal fusion system aligning pitch spectrograms with BERT text embeddings for emotion recognition, achieving 92.3% accuracy on 5 emotional states.

## Q: Does he have industry publications or only academic?
A: Peer-reviewed IEEE publications only. No industry white papers or magazine articles listed.

## Q: Has he presented at conferences?
A: Yes, at IEEE ICCIT (2023, 2025) and IEEE ECCE (2025).

## Q: Does he have patents?
A: No patents listed.

## Q: What is his research philosophy?
A: Combine rigorous methodology with real-world validation. He prefers reproducibility and clinical/statistical rigor over chasing SOTA headlines.

## Q: How does he choose research topics?
A: He picks problems at the intersection of AI capabilities and real human need — especially under-served areas like mental health and AI safety.

## Q: What datasets has he worked with?
