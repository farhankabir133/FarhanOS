# FAQ — Skills & Technologies

## Q: What AI/ML frameworks does Farhan use?
A: PyTorch for training, Hugging Face Transformers (BERT/RoBERTa/Wav2Vec) for NLP and speech, LangChain for RAG orchestration, and Groq API for low-latency inference.

## Q: What frontend technologies does he prefer?
A: React 18/19, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Three.js, Monaco Editor, and Vite.

## Q: What backend technologies does he prefer?
A: Node.js/Express for web APIs, FastAPI for ML serving, Go for high-throughput services, Redis/BullMQ for async queues, and GraphQL where flexible fetching is needed.

## Q: What databases does he use?
A: PostgreSQL and Supabase for relational data, Redis for caching and queueing, ChromaDB for vector search in RAG, and PostGIS for geospatial queries.

## Q: What cloud and DevOps tools does he use?
A: Docker for containerization, GCP for ML training, Vercel for frontend deployment, Railway for backend services, and Linux/Git for day-to-day development.

## Q: Is he more specialized in AI or full-stack?
A: He is full-stack by practice and AI-specialized by research. His unique value is operating fluently across both domains rather than being siloed.

## Q: What NLP tasks has he worked on?
A: Depression detection, emotion detection, adversarial prompt detection, interview evaluation (NLP+CV fusion), and RAG retrieval optimization.

## Q: What computer vision experience does he have?
A: MediaPipe integration in the interview system, spectrogram feature extraction for multimodal emotion recognition, and posture tracking for candidate evaluation.

## Q: Does he use TypeScript?
A: Yes, in Exam-Survival, FarhanOS, and codelab-ai-genkit-rag. He prefers TypeScript for large React codebases to prevent runtime errors.

## Q: What state management does he use?
A: React built-in state and Context for smaller apps; he avoids heavy state libraries unless the app demands it. FarhanOS uses a lightweight custom state machine.

## Q: What testing tools does he use?
A: Jest/Vitest for frontend, pytest for Python, and Postman/automated API tests for backend endpoints.

## Q: Does he use microservices?
A: He separates concerns (e.g., FastAPI for ML, Express for web, Go for telemetry) but keeps deployment simple through containerization rather than full microservice orchestration.

## Q: What is his experience with Docker?
A: Expert-level. He builds reproducible environments for ML training and web services, using Docker Compose for local orchestration and cloud runtime for production.

## Q: What is his experience with Kubernetes?
A: Shallow. He uses it for basic deployments but is not a platform engineer. He prefers Vercel and Railway for managed infrastructure.

## Q: Does he know mobile development?
A: He builds responsive PWAs (Exam-Survival) but does not do native iOS/Android development.

## Q: What design tools does he know?
A: He designs in-code with Tailwind and Framer Motion. Figma expertise is limited; he relies on rapid prototyping and component-driven design.

## Q: Does he do data engineering?
A: Yes, he builds data pipelines for ML (RankFlow AI) and manages metadata, vector storage, and ETL for RAG systems.

## Q: What AI APIs has he integrated?
A: Groq API, Hugging Face Inference API, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and custom FastAPI ML endpoints.

## Q: What is his experience with LLM evaluation?
A: He has published on adversarial robustness, built enterprise observability for 40+ models, and designed RAG systems with hybrid retrieval.

## Q: What soft skills does he bring?
A: Clear technical writing (IEEE papers), cross-functional communication, ownership mentality, and a builder's speed without sacrificing rigor.
